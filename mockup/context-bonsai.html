Reading the tree

Foliage
How much of the context window is in use. A bare trunk is a fresh session.
Leaf color
Green has room. Amber means finish the current step and compact. Red, with leaves falling, means auto-compaction is close.
Wild shoots
Long thin branches past the silhouette show up after about two-thirds full: file dumps, tool output and old detail.
Leaves under the pot
What the last compaction dropped. The pile stays until the next prune.
Trunk and pot marks
The trunk thickens and the pot gets a tally mark each time this session is compacted.

Numbers here are examples and assume a 1M-token window. The real widget would read each turn's token count from the session transcript. The post-compact hook you installed would trigger the prune, and the tree would regrow from the size of the summary plus the rehydrate block.
